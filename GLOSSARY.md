# OIPay

OIPay helps friends, couples, housemates, and travel groups share expenses and keep track of repayments.

## Language

**Group**:
A set of people who track shared expenses together, such as a household or a holiday group.
_Avoid_: Room

**Member**:
A person represented in a particular group.

**Guest member**:
A member who joins through an invitation link using a name, without first signing up for an account.

**Group owner**:
A member with an account who manages the group and its membership.

**Group currency**:
The currency in which a group's expenses, shares, and debts are accounted for.

**Display currency**:
A member's optional currency for viewing equivalent amounts without changing the group's recorded debts.

**Foreign-currency expense**:
An expense entered in another currency and converted to the group currency using a saved exchange rate.

**Expense**:
A cost paid on behalf of people in a group, with an agreed allocation of who owes what.

**Share**:
The portion of an expense assigned to a member.

**Repayment**:
Money paid from one member to another outside OIPay and recorded in the app to update what they owe.
_Avoid_: In-app transfer
